Problem Statement: Problem 2: Build an AI Lawyer

1. Problem Choice & Framing

Why Everyday Disputes?

Every year in India, millions of citizens abandon legitimate claims—withheld rental security deposits (₹30,000–₹80,000), rejected e-commerce refunds (₹5,000–₹40,000), and unpaid wages upon resignation (₹50,000–₹2,00,000). People give up because formal legal consultation feels expensive, legal jargon is intimidating, and procedural steps are unclear.

Conversational Design

Rather than a static web form, NyayaMitra provides a conversational intake workflow: 1. Multilingual Intake (Voice + Text across 11 Languages): Transcribes voice via Whisper Large v3 Turbo or accepts text in Hindi, Hinglish, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Malayalam, Punjabi, and English. 2. Empathetic Dialogue & Fact Extraction: Organizes unstructured explanations into a factual dossier with dates, monetary claims, and documentary proofs. 3. Live Intelligence Sidecar: Real-time side panel displaying the case timeline, evidence checklist, and statutory provisions. 4. Actionable Outputs: Generates a structured 15-day formal Legal Demand Notice and provides an interactive Dispute Practice Arena for mock negotiation.

Scoped Civil Domains

To maintain legal integrity, NyayaMitra is explicitly restricted to three everyday civil domains:
  • Tenancy & Rental Disputes (Model Tenancy Act 2021, Transfer of Property Act Sec 108)
  • Consumer & E-Commerce Disputes (Consumer Protection Act 2019)
  • Employment & Wage Settlement (Code on Wages 2019)

    Deterministic Code vs. LLM Responsibilities

  • Deterministic Code:
    • - Advocate Escalation Filter: Whole-word regex checks for violence, assault, murder, arrest, and bail. Immediately directs high-risk situations to the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA Helpline: 15100). - Closed Statutory Grounding: Only provisions present in the curated local statutory database are supplied to the model. - Regex Section Guard: Scans generated text against allowed section citations and strips unverified sections with a visible warning [unverified reference removed]. - Honest Out-of-Scope Refusal: Unmatched domains (e.g., divorce, criminal defense, property partition) trigger an immediate refusal without guessing.
  • LLM Responsibilities:
    • - Parsing conversational descriptions into timeline events and monetary claims. - Explaining statutory rights in accessible, conversational language. - Formatting formal legal notices and simulating opposing party counter-arguments in the practice arena.

    3. Hallucination Defense & Quality Verification

    1. No Invented Precedents: Judicial case citations were intentionally eliminated from the database to prevent manufactured citations. 2. Code-Level Section & Act Guard: Verifies every statutory section cited against a whitelist extracted exclusively from verified entries in the local knowledge base, and bans repealed/unverified Act names (Payment of Wages Act, Minimum Wages Act, Payment of Bonus Act, Equal Remuneration Act, Indian Penal Code, IPC, CrPC) replacing them with [unverified reference removed]. 3. Repealed Statute Removal: The repealed Payment of Wages Act, 1936 was replaced with the Code on Wages, 2019 (Sections 17(1), 17(2), 17(4), 18(1), 18(2), 18(3)). 4. Automated Verification Suite (verify_test_suite.py): Runs 19 benchmark test cases (10 core civil scenarios + 9 adversarial queries covering invented sections, repealed statutes, criminal threats, maintenance disambiguation, and out-of-scope overrides), achieving a pass count of 19/19 passed (0 failed), logged to test_cases/verification_run.txt. - What the Suite Measures: (a) Code-level section and banned Act guards, (b) Immediate advocate escalation triggers, and (c) Honest out-of-scope refusal. - Note on Answer Correctness: Full answer correctness was checked only on cases where the user filled in expected_answer_verified_by_me. - Raw Model Output Reduction: Across raw model outputs (TC-01 to TC-03), unverified section references were reduced from before fix: 16 unverified sections (5 in TC-01, 4 in TC-02, 7 in TC-03) to after fix: 0 unverified sections, and all banned Act citations were successfully intercepted.

    5. Honest Limitations

  • Model Law Enforceability: The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 is a central model law; it only binds states that have formally enacted it. Local state rent control legislations govern non-adopting states.
  • State-Specific Employment Rules: Timelines for full-and-final settlement under state Shops & Establishments Acts vary across jurisdictions.
  • Crude Regex Scanner: The code-level section guard uses regular expressions. While effective against standard formats (e.g., "Section 17"), it does not recognize spelled-out numbers ("Section seventeen"), Roman numerals, or cross-act section mismatches.
  • Restricted Coverage (3 Topics Only): Only tenancy, consumer disputes, and wage claims are supported. Family law, criminal defense, motor accidents, and corporate litigation are outside scope.
  • No Live Case-Law Search: NyayaMitra does not search live court judgment databases or cite active bench precedents.
  • Static Knowledge Base: The local statutory database is small, hand-curated, and marked with verification statuses (needs_my_verification / verified), source URLs, and verification dates.
  • Information Only: NyayaMitra provides pre-litigation structuring and information; it is not a licensed advocate and cannot represent individuals in court.
