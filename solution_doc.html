1. Problem Choice & Framing

Why Problem 2?

Every year in India, millions of ordinary citizens abandon legitimate claims—unreturned rental security deposits (₹30,000–₹80,000), e-commerce refund refusals (₹5,000–₹40,000), and withheld salaries upon resignation (₹50,000–₹2,00,000). People give up because hiring an advocate feels cost-prohibitive (₹15,000+), legal drafting is intimidating, and the procedural paperwork seems overwhelming.

The True Conversational Solution

Rather than a rigid, static web form or offline batch report generator, NyayaMitra is designed as a genuine, empathetic Conversational AI Legal Partner: 1. Pan-India Multimodal Intake (Voice + Text across 11+ Languages): Takes voice input via browser microphone (transcribed via Whisper Large v3 Turbo) or text across Hindi (हिन्दी), Hinglish, Tamil (தமிழ்), Telugu (తెలుగు), Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), Bengali (বাংলা), Marathi (मराठी), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), Malayalam (മലയാളം), Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ), and English. 2. Empathetic Dialogue: Listens to the user's messy story, extracts key facts organically, and reassures them. 3. Live Case Intelligence Sidecar: As the conversation progresses, a real-time side panel continuously updates the Case Dossier, Chronological Timeline, and Evidence Strength Score (1–10). 4. Actionable Outputs: Generates a ready-to-serve 15-day Formal Legal Demand Notice and provides a Dispute Practice Arena where the user can roleplay against the opposing party.

Scoping the Problem (Depth over Superficial Breadth)

We explicitly scope NyayaMitra to the top 3 everyday civil and consumer disputes in India: Tenancy & Rental Disputes (Model Tenancy Act 2021, Transfer of Property Act Sec 108*) Consumer & E-Commerce Disputes (Consumer Protection Act 2019, e-Daakhil*) Employment & Full-and-Final Dues (Payment of Wages Act 1936, State Shops & Establishments Acts*)

What We Gave to AI vs. What We Kept as Deterministic Rules

  • Kept as Code / Deterministic Rules:
    • - Escalation Boundary Filter: Regex and keyword checks for physical violence, arrest, bail, and matrimonial disputes. If triggered, the AI immediately flags a warning and connects the user to the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA Helpline: 15100). - Statute Retrieval (Closed RAG): Direct retrieval from curated, verified statutory text (Model Tenancy Act, Consumer Protection Act 2019, Payment of Wages Act) to ensure zero hallucinations. - Evidence Scoring: Rule-based verification matrix cross-referencing documentary proof (bank statements, chat logs, job sheets).
  • Gave to AI (LLM):
    • - Parsing messy unstructured voice transcripts and Hinglish prose into chronological events. - Translating legal doctrine into compassionate, empowering advice. - Generating formal legal notices with structured prayer and demand clauses. - Dynamic opponent roleplay with tactical negotiation coaching.

  • Compared to a traditional advocate consultation (₹1,500–₹5,000), NyayaMitra achieves a 99.9% cost reduction, running easily on standard free tiers.

    • 3. Quality Checking & Hallucination Defense

    How Legal Models Hallucinate

    Standard LLMs hallucinate in two dangerous ways: 1. Inventing Fake Sections: E.g., quoting "IPC Section 73 for Breach of Contract" (IPC 73 is solitary confinement; breach of contract is Contract Act Sec 73). 2. Fabricating High Court Case Laws: Manufacturing plausible-sounding bench citations that do not exist.

    NyayaMitra's 3-Layer Hallucination Defense

    1. Constrained Statutory Grounding (Closed RAG): The Legal Advisor is bound to verified Indian enactments (Model Tenancy Act, Consumer Protection Act 2019, Payment of Wages Act). 2. Negative Constraint System Prompts: Agents are explicitly instructed: "Never invent an Act or Section number. If a specific section is not confirmed in the verified context, state the legal principle in plain English without manufacturing a section." 3. Civil vs. Criminal Separation: Prevents users from making frivolous criminal cheating (IPC 420 / BNS 318) threats for civil breaches, following Supreme Court precedent (Dalip Kaur v. Jagnar Singh).

    5. Honest Limitations & Where the Solution Fails

  • State-Specific Rent Laws: While the Model Tenancy Act provides national guidance, legacy rent control acts (e.g., Maharashtra Rent Control Act 1999) have localized procedural quirks.
  • Disputed Verbal Agreements: When a user has zero written proof, rent agreement, or chat history, the AI can only recommend issuing an exploratory demand notice; it cannot manufacture proof.
  • Criminal Matters: The AI will not assist in criminal bail, cross-examinations, or courtroom trials; it strictly enforces handoff to a human advocate.
